# Devnet demo wallets

Throwaway wallets for demos on Solana **devnet** only. Fund each from https://faucet.solana.com
(paste its address, pick devnet). Never send real SOL to them: the private keys are below.

- `wallets/wallet-N.json`: Solana CLI keypair (`solana balance wallets/wallet-1.json --url devnet`)
- Private key (base58): add it as your account's wallet on the nodes page, or paste it when `sinference up` asks
